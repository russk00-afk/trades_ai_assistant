Trades AI Assistant

An AI-powered job management assistant designed to help tradespeople save time on paperwork, check supplier invoices, review quotes, and avoid overlooking work that may need to be charged to customers.

The Problem

Tradespeople spend too much time on paperwork after a long working day. Extra labour and materials can be forgotten when preparing invoices, and supplier invoices and customer quotes can contain errors or unclear details.

Our Solution

A simple AI assistant that helps tradespeople organise job information and review costs before sending invoices or accepting quotes.

Planned Features

1. Supplier Invoice Checker — Extract invoice details, check arithmetic and totals, and flag possible discrepancies for review.
2. Quote Checker — Identify unclear scope, missing cost categories, calculation errors, and potential risks.
3. AI Voice Notes — Convert spoken job notes into structured records of labour, materials, problems discovered, and additional work.
4. Job-to-Invoice Checker — Compare the original quote with completed-work notes and available receipts to flag potential unbilled extras.
5. Invoice Draft Assistant — Prepare a suggested invoice breakdown and customer message for the tradesperson to review and approve.

How It Should Work

1. Create or select a job.
2. Add the original quote and job details.
3. Type notes or, in a later version, record voice notes.
4. Add relevant receipts, photos, or supplier invoices.
5. Review AI findings and potential extra charges.
6. Approve and use the suggested invoice information.

Our Principles

- Keep the product simple and practical.
- The tradesperson always makes the final decision.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed arithmetic errors from possible discrepancies.
- Never assume extra work is automatically billable.
- Protect customer documents and business information.
- Build and test a small useful version before adding advanced features.

Initial Target Customers

Independent tradespeople and small trade businesses that want less paperwork and better visibility of job costs.
